Settings & protection details

The switch controls both text and files. This preference is saved locally. Reversible token mappings stay only in the browser session vault.

Choose findings, then Protect Selected or Protect File. IDIA never clicks Send. A website may upload a file once the protected copy is ready.

PDFs: up to 25 MiB and 50 pages. Images: 25 MiB, 8 million decoded pixels, 8192 pixels per dimension. Text/Office files: 5 MiB. Large PDF jobs ask before scanning; more than 25 OCR pages triggers a warning. PDF protection covers selected regions and makes image-only pages. Text/TXT/CSV/DOCX/XLSX use reversible session tokens. PDF/image redactions cannot be decoded. OCR can miss information; inspect the protected copy. PNG/JPEG output removes source metadata and flattens transparency onto white. JPEG uses quality 0.94. HEIC/HEIF/TIFF and drag-and-drop are unsupported.
